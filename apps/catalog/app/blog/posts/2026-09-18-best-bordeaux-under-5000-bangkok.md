## Why This Price Band Is Bordeaux's Sweet Spot

Above ฿5,000, you're paying for scarcity and cellar-worthiness — wines built to be judged in 2040, like the first growths in our [Château Margaux guide](/blog/chateau-margaux-bangkok-guide). Below it live the wines Bordeaux actually drinks: classified growths from less fashionable communes, brilliant second labels, and the huge Saint-Émilion Grand Cru category, mostly ready to enjoy tonight with nothing more than an hour in a decanter.

One decoding note before the bottles. "Grand Cru Classé" means different things on each bank: in the Médoc it's the strict 1855 ladder of 61 châteaux; in Saint-Émilion it's a broader, regularly revised classification. Neither is a synonym for expensive — as the list below proves. Our [wine designations guide](/blog/wine-designations-explained-grand-cru-reserva-docg) untangles the full vocabulary.

## How to Choose

Cabernet-led Left Bank (Branaire-Ducru, Talbot, Baron de Brane) wants red meat, structure, and a decanter; Merlot-led Right Bank (the Saint-Émilions) is softer, friendlier, and readier on opening night. ## FAQ

### What is the best Bordeaux under 5000 baht in Bangkok?
Domaine de Chevalier Pessac-Léognan 2020 (฿4,558, 99 points) is the strongest wine in the band right now, with Château Branaire-Ducru Saint-Julien 2018 (฿4,100, 97 points) close behind. For value, Château de Ferrand Saint-Émilion Grand Cru Classé on sale at ฿1,958 is exceptional.

### Can you get real classed-growth Bordeaux under ฿5,000?
Yes. Both Saint-Julien fourth growths (Talbot, Branaire-Ducru), a Margaux second growth's second wine (Baron de Brane), and Saint-Émilion Grand Cru Classé bottles (Château de Ferrand) all sit under ฿5,000 in Bangkok since the 2024 wine tax cuts.

### What's the difference between Left Bank and Right Bank Bordeaux?
The Left Bank (Médoc, Graves) leads with Cabernet Sauvignon — structured, tannic, cedar-and-cassis wines that reward decanting and food. The Right Bank (Saint-Émilion, Pomerol) leads with Merlot — plusher, rounder wines that drink well younger.

### Does "Grand Cru" on a Saint-Émilion label mean the same as in the Médoc?
No. The Médoc's 1855 classification is a fixed ladder of 61 châteaux; Saint-Émilion's classification is broader and revised roughly every decade, with "Grand Cru" alone being a large appellation tier below "Grand Cru Classé." It's why a Saint-Émilion Grand Cru can cost ฿1,060 — and still overdeliver.

### Should I decant these wines?
The Left Bank bottles benefit from 45–60 minutes in a decanter, especially the young Branaire-Ducru 2018. The Right Bank Merlots and Mouton Cadet are ready on pouring, though ten minutes of air never hurts a young Bordeaux.
